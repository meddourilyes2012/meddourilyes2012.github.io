✨ What is FaceMac?

FaceMac is a clever little application that brings the magic of Face ID to your Mac. Instead of typing your password every time your Mac goes to sleep or locks, you simply look at your screen — and FaceMac automatically types your saved password for you.

It uses advanced on-device facial recognition technology powered by Apple's CoreML framework and the SFace algorithm — a lightweight and efficient face recognition model. This means everything happens 100% locally on your Mac. Your face data never leaves your computer, and no images are ever uploaded anywhere.

It even replicates the iconic Face ID notch animation that you know from modern iPhones, making the unlock experience feel seamless and premium.

⚙️ How It Works

  1. Setup: Once you download and launch FaceMac, it will guide you through a simple face enrollment process.
  2. Lock Screen: When your Mac's screen locks (either manually or automatically), FaceMac activates and starts its camera.
  3. Recognition: It uses your Mac's camera to see your face and instantly compares it to your saved face using on-device recognition.
  4. Auto-Unlock: Once it matches, FaceMac automatically types your saved Mac password and presses Enter — unlocking your computer instantly.
  5. Security: The app cannot be tricked by photos or 2D images, as SFace uses advanced depth-aware detection.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe to use?

Absolutely. FaceMac is open-source, which means anyone can inspect its code. It doesn't have access to your files or data — it only types your password at the login screen and runs its recognition locally.

What if it can't recognize me in the dark?

SFace uses visible light camera imaging. If you're in a completely dark room, you may need to rely on your password. For best results, have a minimal light source directed toward your face.

Will it work with a virtual machine or external display?

FaceMac works with any camera your Mac can see. If you're using an external webcam connected to your Mac, it should work fine. Virtual machines are not officially supported, but it's best to use the built-in camera for optimal performance.

Can I use FaceMac with multiple users?

Yes! Each user on the Mac can set up their own face with FaceMac. The app will detect who is sitting in front of it and unlock the respective account.
